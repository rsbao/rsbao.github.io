What is freedom? There are many kinds of freedom one can achieve. I will group them into financial freedom, time freedom, and action freedom.

I define Financial freedom as the ability to live without worrying about money. This is derived from two factors: your income and your lifestyle. One can achieve financial freedom by increasing income, decreasing living costs, or both. The option which modern society pushes us towards is the former. Luxury items, useless gadgets and trinkets, bigger houses, faster cars: these are the temptations which blind everyone from the option of “settling” for a modest, cost-efficient lifestyle. 

Time freedom is the ability to spend your time on whatever your please, whenever you please. 

The result of consumerism is people working longer hours for more pay to increase their standard of living, sacrificing their time freedom for the illusion of financial freedom. Note that a low standard of living was never the issue. People in medival times with no running water or abundant food were able to be happy. It is comparison which kills us. Unfortunately, it seems that it is human nature which compels us to strive for status. Survival is not enough.

Action freedom is the ability to control what you do and when you do it.

Someone says to you, “i cant work for someone, thats for npcs. I’m gonna found my own startup so I can be my own boss.” This is an example of a freedom configuration which emphasizes action freedom over potentially both financial and time freedom. However, as their startup scales and gains investment, they may lose this action freedom as investors become the boss. If they have reached this stage, then the tradeoff space has already shifted to more financial freedom at the expense of the other two. We can see that some people (known as serial founders) prefer the initial configuration, leaving companies once their freedom configuration is forced to change. Before you give life advice to someone, take into consideration their preferred freedom configuration.

How do you know when your life has devolved into a financial freedom-maxxing s-hole? You feel guilty for even having free time. You feel that any time or action not spent making money or working towards making money is a waste. This is the life of many of the people I know. This is a sad existence. For these people, time and action freedom are unattainable.
